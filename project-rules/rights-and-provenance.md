# Rights and Provenance Rules

- Preserve all upstream author, copyright, and license notices in source files and bundled components.
- Product branding as metisa by SaGaLabs does not establish ownership or transfer rights in inherited Arduino Pedelec/Forumscontroller code.
- The root `LICENSE` is a draft for legal review, not an operative blanket grant or worldwide enforceability opinion. Do not describe the repository as legally cleared.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` is preliminary. In particular, HX711's included GPLv2 text and MenuSystem's conflicting public-domain/LGPL notices are unresolved release blockers.
- Do not replace third-party terms with a metisa-only private/commercial restriction. A restrictive agreement may cover only rights verified as held or licensed by the signing party; third-party grants remain applicable.
- For each new code, library, font, image, design file, or other asset, record source, version, author, license, and required notice before inclusion.
- Do not make legal conclusions about copyright assignment, GPL compatibility, trademark, patents, or utility-model protection. Escalate those questions to qualified counsel and relevant rights holders.
- Until the rights chain is reviewed, do not publish or redistribute the combined source/binary as legally cleared. Private technical preparation does not cure a rights gap.
